Dark Matter Park (1x1 Asset)
Citizens are inexplicably drawn to this mesmerizing pocket park, where art, motion, and light come together in a hypnotic dance. 
At its heart stands a kinetic sculpture — three illuminated rings rotating gracefully around intersecting axes above a minimalist plinth. The sculpture glows brilliantly day and night, adding a futuristic aesthetic to any cityscape. 
This 1×1 park does not require a direct street connection, though the sculpture needs electricity to power its lights and motion. If placed standalone, remember to provide nearby road access for park maintenance vehicles. 
Position it near a shoreline to create a striking light beacon visible from afar — a glowing landmark for citizens and travelers alike.
Inspiration:
Inspired by the mysterious machine from the film *Contact*, the Dark Matter incubator from *Egg, Inc.*, the kinetic ring lights by [WHITEvoid] (Germany), and Christopher Bauder’s *Circular* installation at *Dark Matter Berlin*.
