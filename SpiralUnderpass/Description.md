Spiral Underpass

Make pedestrian networks seamless and stylish with this compact 1x1 Spiral Underpass module designed for connecting underground pathways to the surface.

Features

- Found under Landscaping → Pathways for easy access.
- Does not need a road connection, but requires electricity for lighting to function.
- Built with a custom spiral stairwell unit for high capacity usage.
- Accurate pedestrian path ensures realistic stair usage.
- Well-lit stairwell for safe, nighttime visibility.
- Small footprint allows flexible landscaping, props, and surface design nearby.
- Essential for busy intersections and wide streets—place at corners of major roads to eliminate crosswalks and keep traffic flowing smoothly. Or place on opposite sides of a wide street for convenient pedestrians crossings.
- Six building-color variations.
- Includes random combinations of street props such as a newspaper dispenser or functional bench.
- Fully tested and operational. This is not just a visual asset. Citizens walk up and down the spiral stairs if connected properly.
- Localized in 12 languages for a global audience.

Usage

- Best used with divided roads where the crosswalks are removed [as shown].
- Remove crosswalks: use the advanced road services and select the crosswalk icon; right-click near the crosswalk.
- Place the stairwell asset and connect it to underground 'pavement paths' at -6.25 meters [or -18.75 feet] depth. A connection ring [positioned at precisely -6.25m] is included for ease of connecting tunnels with other assets. A correctly attached tunnel is flat. It is best to approach the connection ring without snapping, and it helps to curve the tunnel if going directly across a divided street.
- A quick method to connect intersections is to place two tunnels diagonally.
- The primary module does not require a road connection - it can be installed along pavement paths to access underground networks. To power the lights, attach electric cable underground or overhead.
- A legacy version of the module containing a tunnel stub is included.
- The entrance is accessible from the front at ground level and through underground tunnels at the base.
- Contains a park component that enables bench utilization and pedestrian passage through the asset for functional realism. No park maintenance is required.
